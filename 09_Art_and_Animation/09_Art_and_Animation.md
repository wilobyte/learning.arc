## Syllabus

### 1. The Absolute Beginning: Form & Perspective (Weeks 1-4)
- **Topics**:
  - Pencil control and line confidence (Drawabox basics)
  - Construction: Spheres, Cubes, Cylinders in perspective
  - The "Mannequin" head and basic face proportions
- **Practice**: Complete the first half of *Fun With a Pencil* (Loomis); Draw 50 boxes in 3-point perspective
- **Applications**: Understanding 3D space on a 2D plane (essential for both Krita and Blender viewport navigation)

### 2. Anatomy and Construction (Weeks 5-12)
- **Topics**:
  - The body as a machine: Interlocking shapes
  - Major muscle groups (Torso, Arms, Legs)
  - Hands and Feet mechanics
  - Volume preservation in rotation
- **Practice**: Study *Figure Drawing: Design and Invention* (Hampton); daily life drawing sessions (1 hr)
- **Applications**: Character design, organic modeling preparation

### 3. The Sakuga Engine: Gesture and Flow (Weeks 13-20)
- **Topics**:
  - Rhythm, Line of Action, and Force curves
  - Drawing "Verbs" vs "Nouns"
  - Exaggeration and Dynamic Posing
  - Silhouette clarity
- **Practice**: *FORCE* (Mattesi) exercises; 30-second gesture drawings; Keyframe posing in Krita
- **Applications**: Rigging deformation, creating dynamic animation keyframes

### 4. Making it Move: Animation Principles (Weeks 21-28)
- **Topics**:
  - Timing and Spacing (Charts)
  - Squash and Stretch
  - Anticipation, Overshoot, and Settle
  - Walk Cycles (2D and 3D)
- **Practice**: *The Animator's Survival Kit* exercises; Bouncing ball, Flour Sack animation
- **Applications**: 2D Anime (Sakuga) and 3D Character Animation

### 5. The 3D Bridge: Topology and Modeling (Weeks 29-36)
- **Topics**:
  - Hard Surface vs. Organic Modeling
  - Edge Loops, Poles, and Quads
  - Retopology for Animation
  - UV Unwrapping logic
- **Practice**: *Topology Workbook* (Vaughan); Model a character bust with clean topology in Blender
- **Applications**: Creating game-ready or film-ready assets that deform correctly

### 6. Texturing, Lighting, and Rendering (Weeks 37-44)
- **Topics**:
  - Light Physics: Diffuse, Specular, Ambient Occlusion
  - Color Scripts and Mood
  - PBR (Physically Based Rendering) vs. NPR (Non-Photorealistic/Anime Rendering)
  - Shader Nodes in Blender
- **Practice**: *Color and Light* (Gurney) studies; Texture the character bust using Krita and Blender
- **Applications**: Visual development, final frame rendering

### 7. Advanced Pipeline: Rigging and FX (Weeks 45-52)
- **Topics**:
  - Bones, Armatures, and Weight Painting
  - Forward Kinematics (FK) vs Inverse Kinematics (IK)
  - Elemental FX (Fire, Water, Smoke) principles
  - Blender Grease Pencil integration
- **Practice**: *Elemental Magic* studies; Rig the character; Animate a short combat sequence with FX
- **Applications**: Full production pipeline, "Sakuga" style finished shots
