## Syllabus  

### 1. LaTeX and Proof Foundations (Weeks 1-2)  
- **Topics**:  
  - LaTeX Basics and Mathematical Typesetting  
  - Proof Techniques and Logical Foundations  
  - Well-Ordering Principle and Induction  
- **Practice**: Write proofs and mathematical documents in LaTeX  
- **Applications**: Formal reasoning and documentation for computer science  

---

### 2. Logic and Mathematical Structures (Weeks 3-4)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Propositional and Predicate Logic  
  - Mathematical Data Types and Structural Recursion  
  - Infinite Sets and Computability Concepts  
- **Practice**: Logical proofs, recursive definitions, and computability exercises  
- **Applications**: Foundations of algorithms and programming languages  

---

### 3. Number Theory and Cryptography (Weeks 5-6)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Number Theory Basics  
  - Cryptographic Principles and Applications  
- **Practice**: Solve problems related to primes, modular arithmetic, and encryption  
- **Applications**: Security and cryptographic protocols in computing  

---

### 4. Graph Theory and Networks (Weeks 7-8)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Directed Graphs, Partial Orders  
  - Communication Networks and Simple Graphs  
  - Planar Graphs and Euler’s Formula  
- **Practice**: Graph algorithms and network modeling  
- **Applications**: Network design, data structures, and distributed systems  

---

### 5. Discrete Mathematics and Probability (Weeks 9-10)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Counting Principles, Generating Functions  
  - Probability Spaces and Random Variables  
  - Deviation, Random Processes, and Gambler’s Ruin  
- **Practice**: Combinatorial problems and probabilistic modeling  
- **Applications**: Algorithm analysis and randomized algorithms  

---

### 6. Algorithms and Automata Theory (Weeks 11-13)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Recurrences and Divide-and-Conquer Algorithms  
  - Finite Automata and Regular Languages  
  - Context-Free Grammars and Pushdown Automata  
  - Turing Machines and Decidability  
  - Reducibility and the Recursion Theorem  
- **Practice**: Design and analyze automata and algorithms  
- **Applications**: Compiler design, language recognition, and computability  

---

### 7. Complexity Theory (Weeks 14-15)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Complexity Classes: P, NP, PSPACE, NL  
  - Advanced Complexity Topics  
- **Practice**: Problem reductions and complexity proofs  
- **Applications**: Computational complexity and algorithmic limits
