## Syllabus  

### 1. Foundations of AI (Weeks 1-2)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Definition of AI  
  - History and Scope of AI  
  - Intelligent Agents  
  - AI Research Trends  
- **Practice**: Analyze AI case studies and applications  
- **Applications**: Autonomous systems, expert systems  

---

### 2. Problem-Solving Techniques (Weeks 3-4)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Search Strategies (Uninformed, Informed)  
  - Adversarial Search and Game Playing  
  - Constraint Satisfaction Problems  
  - Optimization Techniques  
- **Practice**: Solve problems using search algorithms  
- **Applications**: Pathfinding, decision-making  

---

### 3. Knowledge Representation and Reasoning (Weeks 5-6)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Propositional and Predicate Logic  
  - Ontologies and Semantic Networks  
  - Knowledge-Based Systems  
  - Reasoning Under Uncertainty  
- **Practice**: Design knowledge bases and implement reasoning systems  
- **Applications**: Natural language understanding, expert systems  

---

### 4. Planning and Decision Making (Weeks 7-8)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Classical Planning Techniques  
  - Heuristics for Planning  
  - Decision Theory  
  - Markov Decision Processes (MDPs)  
- **Practice**: Create plans for complex tasks  
- **Applications**: Robotics, scheduling  

---

### 5. Uncertainty in AI (Weeks 9-10)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Bayesian Networks  
  - Probabilistic Reasoning  
  - Hidden Markov Models (HMMs)  
  - Fuzzy Logic  
- **Practice**: Solve problems using probabilistic models  
- **Applications**: Speech recognition, recommendation systems  

---

### 6. Machine Learning Fundamentals (Weeks 11-12)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Supervised Learning (Regression, Classification)  
  - Unsupervised Learning (Clustering, Dimensionality Reduction)  
  - Model Evaluation and Validation  
  - Feature Engineering  
- **Practice**: Train machine learning models  
- **Applications**: Data analytics, predictive modeling  

---

### 7. Neural Networks and Deep Learning (Weeks 13-14)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Basics of Neural Networks  
  - Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)  
  - Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)  
  - Optimization Techniques in Deep Learning  
- **Practice**: Implement neural networks for image and sequence data  
- **Applications**: Computer vision, time-series analysis  

---

### 8. Natural Language Processing (Weeks 15-16)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Tokenization and Text Preprocessing  
  - Word Embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe)  
  - Sequence-to-Sequence Models  
  - Applications (Sentiment Analysis, Machine Translation)  
- **Practice**: Build NLP pipelines and train models  
- **Applications**: Language understanding, chatbots  

---

### 9. Robotics and Perception (Weeks 17-18)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Perception Systems (Sensors, Computer Vision)  
  - Motion and Path Planning  
  - Robot Control Architectures  
  - Human-Robot Interaction  
- **Practice**: Simulate robotic tasks and control systems  
- **Applications**: Autonomous vehicles, industrial robots  

---

### 10. Multi-Agent Systems (Weeks 19-20)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Coordination and Cooperation  
  - Game Theory in AI  
  - Distributed AI  
  - Applications of Multi-Agent Systems  
- **Practice**: Design and analyze multi-agent simulations  
- **Applications**: Networked systems, collaborative robotics  

---

### 11. Ethics and Future of AI (Weeks 21-22)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Ethical Considerations in AI Development  
  - AI and Job Automation  
  - Safety and Accountability  
  - AI for Social Good  
- **Practice**: Write essays on AI ethics and present case studies  
- **Applications**: Policy development, ethical AI design-e
