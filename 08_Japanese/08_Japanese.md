## Syllabus  

### 1. Introduction & Writing System (Weeks 1–2)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Overview of Japanese language  
  - Hiragana chart and usage  
  - Katakana chart and usage  
  - Introduction to Kanji  
- **Practice**:  
  - Writing and reading hiragana and katakana  
  - Basic Kanji recognition  
- **Applications**:  
  - Reading simple Japanese texts  
  - Writing basic words and greetings  

---

### 2. Basic Grammar (Weeks 3–6)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Expressing state-of-being  
  - Particles は、も、が  
  - Adjectives and their forms  
  - Verb basics and conjugations  
  - Negative verbs and past tense  
  - Verb-related particles を、に、へ、で  
  - Transitive vs intransitive verbs  
  - Relative clauses and sentence order  
  - Noun-related particles と、や、とか、の  
  - Adverbs and sentence-ending particles  
- **Practice**:  
  - Constructing simple sentences  
  - Particle usage drills  
  - Verb conjugation exercises  
- **Applications**:  
  - Everyday conversation  
  - Describing people, places, and actions  

---

### 3. Essential Grammar (Weeks 7–12)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Polite forms ～です、～ます  
  - Addressing people and question marker か  
  - Compound sentences with て-form and conjunctions  
  - Uses of the te-form  
  - Potential form and conditionals  
  - Expressing necessity and obligation  
  - Desire and suggestions  
  - Giving and receiving expressions  
  - Making requests and commands  
- **Practice**:  
  - Dialogue practice  
  - Writing compound sentences  
  - Role-playing polite requests  
- **Applications**:  
  - Formal and informal conversations  
  - Expressing intentions and requests  

---

### 4. Numbers, Casual Speech & Special Expressions (Weeks 13–16)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Numbers and counting systems  
  - Casual speech patterns and slang  
  - Sentence-ending particles review  
  - Causative and passive forms  
  - Honorific and humble language  
  - Expressing unintentional actions  
  - Certainty, amounts, similarity, and hearsay expressions  
  - Comparison structures and ease/difficulty expressions  
- **Practice**:  
  - Listening to casual dialogues  
  - Practicing honorific and humble forms  
  - Using special expressions in sentences  
- **Applications**:  
  - Social conversations  
  - Understanding spoken Japanese in informal settings  

---

### 5. Advanced Topics (Weeks 17–20)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Formal expressions and expectations  
  - Signs and tendencies in language  
  - Advanced volitional forms  
  - Expressions of proximity and timing  
  - Miscellaneous advanced grammar points  
- **Practice**:  
  - Reading formal texts  
  - Writing essays using advanced grammar  
- **Applications**:  
  - Academic and professional Japanese  
  - Advanced reading comprehension
