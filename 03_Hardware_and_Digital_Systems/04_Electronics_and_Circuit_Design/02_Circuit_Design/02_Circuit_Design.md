## Syllabus

### 1. Circuit Elements and Analysis (Weeks 1-2)
- **Topics**:
  - Types of Circuit Elements (Resistors, Capacitors, Inductors)
  - Ohm's Law and Power
  - Voltage and Current Dividers
  - Basic Series and Parallel Circuits
  - Superposition and Thevenin’s Theorem
- **Practice**: Solve problems involving Ohm’s Law, voltage, and current dividers
- **Applications**: Basic electrical systems, initial understanding of components used in circuits

---

### 2. Kirchhoff's Laws (Weeks 3-4)
- **Topics**:
  - Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)
  - Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL)
  - Application of KVL and KCL in solving circuits
  - Mesh and Nodal Analysis
- **Practice**: Apply Kirchhoff’s laws to solve circuit problems
- **Applications**: Circuit analysis for power distribution and network design

---

### 3. Network Theorems (Weeks 5-6)
- **Topics**:
  - Thevenin and Norton Theorems
  - Maximum Power Transfer Theorem
  - Superposition Theorem
  - Millman’s Theorem
- **Practice**: Use network theorems to simplify circuits for easier analysis
- **Applications**: Practical applications in simplifying real-world electrical systems

---

### 4. AC Circuit Analysis (Weeks 7-8)
- **Topics**:
  - Sinusoidal Waveforms and Phasors
  - Impedance and Reactance
  - AC Power and Power Factor
  - RLC Circuits and Resonance
- **Practice**: Solve problems involving impedance and AC circuit power
- **Applications**: Signal processing, electrical power transmission

---

### 5. Transients and Frequency Response (Weeks 9-10)
- **Topics**:
  - Time Domain Analysis of First-Order and Second-Order Circuits
  - Natural Response and Forced Response
  - Frequency Response Analysis
  - Bode Plots and Transfer Functions
- **Practice**: Analyze transients and frequency responses in different circuit configurations
- **Applications**: Circuit design for communication systems and filters

---

### 6. Operational Amplifiers (Weeks 11-12)
- **Topics**:
  - Ideal and Real Operational Amplifiers
  - Inverting and Non-Inverting Amplifiers
  - Differential Amplifiers
  - Voltage Follower and Buffer Circuits
  - Active Filters and Oscillators
- **Practice**: Design and analyze circuits with operational amplifiers
- **Applications**: Signal conditioning, instrumentation

---

### 7. Filters and Resonance (Weeks 13-14)
- **Topics**:
  - Low-Pass, High-Pass, Band-Pass, and Band-Stop Filters
  - Filter Design and Transfer Functions
  - Resonance in RLC Circuits
  - Quality Factor and Damping
- **Practice**: Design filters for frequency selection and resonance
- **Applications**: Audio systems, communication systems

---

### 8. Power Analysis and Efficiency (Weeks 15-16)
- **Topics**:
  - Active, Reactive, and Apparent Power
  - Power Factor Correction
  - Power Efficiency in AC Circuits
  - Power Factor in Three-Phase Systems
- **Practice**: Analyze power distribution in electrical circuits
- **Applications**: Electrical power systems, optimization of electrical devices

---

### 9. Laplace Transform and Circuit Solutions (Weeks 17-18)
- **Topics**:
  - Introduction to Laplace Transforms in Circuit Analysis
  - Solving Circuits Using Laplace Transforms
  - Transfer Functions and System Stability
  - Inverse Laplace Transforms for Time-Domain Solutions
- **Practice**: Solve circuits with Laplace transforms and inverse transforms
- **Applications**: Control systems, signal processing

---

### 10. Fourier Analysis (Weeks 19-20)
- **Topics**:
  - Fourier Series and Fourier Transform
  - Frequency Domain Analysis
  - Signal Decomposition into Sinusoids
  - Fourier Transform in Circuit Design
- **Practice**: Analyze signals using Fourier transforms
- **Applications**: Communication systems, audio processing

---

### 11. Signal Processing and Applications (Weeks 21-22)
- **Topics**:
  - Introduction to Analog and Digital Signal Processing
  - Sampling Theorem
  - Fourier Transform in Signal Processing
  - Filters in Signal Processing (Low, High, Band-Pass)
- **Practice**: Implement signal processing algorithms and analyze signals
- **Applications**: Digital communication, audio, and video processing-e
