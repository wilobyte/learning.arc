## Syllabus

### 1. Fundamentals of Electronics (Weeks 1-2)
- **Topics**:  
  - Atomic Structure and Electrical Conductivity  
  - Ohm's Law and Kirchhoff's Laws  
  - Power, Energy, and Circuit Elements  
  - Series and Parallel Circuits  
  - Voltage, Current, and Resistance  
- **Practice**: Solve basic circuit problems, analyze simple circuits  
- **Applications**: Understanding basic electrical circuits, foundation for all electronics topics  

---

### 2. Semiconductors and Diodes (Weeks 3-4)
- **Topics**:  
  - Semiconductor Materials and Properties  
  - P-N Junction and Diodes  
  - Diode Characteristics and Applications  
  - Zener Diodes and Rectifiers  
  - LED, Photodiodes, and Solar Cells  
- **Practice**: Analyze diode circuits, calculate diode parameters  
- **Applications**: Power rectification, signal modulation, light emission  

---

### 3. Transistors and Amplifiers (Weeks 5-6)
- **Topics**:  
  - Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT)  
  - Field-Effect Transistors (FET)  
  - Amplification and Signal Processing  
  - Common-Emitter and Common-Collector Configurations  
  - Transistor Biasing and Stability  
- **Practice**: Design and analyze transistor amplifiers  
- **Applications**: Signal amplification, audio and RF circuits  

---

### 4. Digital Electronics and Logic Circuits (Weeks 7-8)
- **Topics**:  
  - Binary Numbers and Logic Gates  
  - Boolean Algebra and Logic Simplification  
  - Combinational Logic Circuits  
  - Sequential Logic Circuits  
  - Flip-Flops, Registers, and Counters  
  - Digital Multiplexers and Decoders  
- **Practice**: Design combinational and sequential circuits  
- **Applications**: Digital circuit design, computer architecture  

---

### 5. Operational Amplifiers (Weeks 9-10)
- **Topics**:  
  - Introduction to Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps)  
  - Inverting and Non-Inverting Amplifiers  
  - Voltage Follower and Differential Amplifiers  
  - Filter Circuits and Integrators  
  - Application of Op-Amps in Signal Conditioning  
- **Practice**: Analyze Op-Amp circuits, design active filters  
- **Applications**: Analog signal processing, instrumentation, audio systems  

---

### 6. Power Electronics (Weeks 11-12)
- **Topics**:  
  - Power Semiconductor Devices (Diodes, Triacs, Thyristors)  
  - Power Conversion Circuits (Rectifiers, Inverters, Choppers)  
  - DC-DC Converters and Switch-Mode Power Supplies  
  - Power Factor Correction  
  - Motor Drives and Control  
- **Practice**: Design and analyze power conversion circuits  
- **Applications**: Power management, renewable energy systems  

---

### 7. Communication Systems (Weeks 13-14)
- **Topics**:  
  - Basic Principles of Communication  
  - Amplitude Modulation (AM) and Frequency Modulation (FM)  
  - Phase Modulation (PM)  
  - Modulator and Demodulator Circuits  
  - Communication Systems Components (Antennas, Transmitters, Receivers)  
- **Practice**: Analyze modulation and demodulation circuits  
- **Applications**: Radio and television broadcasting, wireless communication  

---

### 8. Signal Processing (Weeks 15-16)
- **Topics**:  
  - Analog Signal Processing Techniques  
  - Frequency Response and Filters  
  - Fourier Transform and Spectral Analysis  
  - Digital Signal Processing Fundamentals  
  - Convolution and Sampling Theorem  
- **Practice**: Implement signal processing techniques  
- **Applications**: Audio processing, image processing, telecommunications  

---

### 9. Electronic Devices and Components (Weeks 17-18)
- **Topics**:  
  - Transistor Characteristics and Applications  
  - Power Diodes, Zener Diodes, and LED Applications  
  - Integrated Circuits and Hybrid Circuits  
  - Sensors and Actuators in Electronics  
  - Capacitors, Inductors, and Resistors in Circuit Design  
- **Practice**: Analyze device specifications and applications  
- **Applications**: Sensor systems, automation, control systems  

---

### 10. Analog and Digital Systems Design (Weeks 19-20)
- **Topics**:  
  - Analog System Design Principles  
  - Digital Circuit Design Principles  
  - Hybrid Analog-Digital Systems  
  - Digital Signal Processors (DSPs) and Microcontrollers  
  - Real-Time System Design  
- **Practice**: Design analog and digital systems  
- **Applications**: Embedded systems, robotics, IoT devices  

---

### 11. Measurement and Testing Techniques (Weeks 21-22)
- **Topics**:  
  - Multimeter Usage and Calibration  
  - Oscilloscope and Signal Generators  
  - Signal Analysis and Fault Diagnosis  
  - Soldering Techniques and Prototyping  
  - Test Equipment for Power Electronics and Digital Circuits  
- **Practice**: Use test equipment for circuit analysis  
- **Applications**: Circuit troubleshooting, product development  

---

### 12. Advanced Electronics Topics (Weeks 23-24)
- **Topics**:  
  - Advanced Op-Amp Circuits  
  - High-Frequency and RF Circuit Design  
  - Microelectronics and VLSI Design  
  - Semiconductor Fabrication Techniques  
  - Quantum Electronics  
- **Practice**: Design high-frequency and advanced circuits  
- **Applications**: Semiconductor industry, communication systems, advanced research-e
