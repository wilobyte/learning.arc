## Syllabus  

### 1. Descriptive Statistics (Weeks 1-2)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Measures of Central Tendency (Mean, Median, Mode)  
  - Measures of Dispersion (Range, Variance, Standard Deviation)  
  - Data Visualization (Histograms, Box Plots, Pie Charts)  
  - Frequency Distributions  
  - Skewness and Kurtosis  
- **Practice**: Calculate and interpret descriptive statistics for datasets  
- **Applications**: Data summarization, data exploration  

---

### 2. Probability Theory (Weeks 3-4)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Basic Probability Concepts  
  - Conditional Probability  
  - Bayes' Theorem  
  - Law of Total Probability  
  - Addition and Multiplication Rules  
- **Practice**: Solve probability problems, use Bayes' Theorem  
- **Applications**: Machine learning, data prediction, reliability analysis  

---

### 3. Random Variables (Weeks 5-6)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Discrete and Continuous Random Variables  
  - Expectation and Variance  
  - Moment Generating Functions  
  - Transformation of Random Variables  
  - Covariance and Correlation  
- **Practice**: Calculate expectations, variances, and covariances  
- **Applications**: Signal processing, communication systems  

---

### 4. Probability Distributions (Weeks 7-8)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Discrete Probability Distributions (Binomial, Poisson)  
  - Continuous Probability Distributions (Normal, Exponential, Uniform)  
  - Central Limit Theorem  
  - Chi-Square Distribution  
- **Practice**: Apply probability distributions to real-world problems  
- **Applications**: Risk analysis, quality control, engineering  

---

### 5. Statistical Inference (Weeks 9-10)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Point Estimation  
  - Confidence Intervals  
  - Maximum Likelihood Estimation  
  - Method of Moments  
- **Practice**: Construct confidence intervals, estimate parameters  
- **Applications**: Data analysis, model estimation  

---

### 6. Hypothesis Testing (Weeks 11-12)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Null and Alternative Hypotheses  
  - Type I and Type II Errors  
  - p-Values and Significance Levels  
  - One-Sample and Two-Sample Tests  
  - ANOVA (Analysis of Variance)  
- **Practice**: Conduct hypothesis tests and interpret results  
- **Applications**: Decision making, experimental research  

---

### 7. Regression Analysis (Weeks 13-14)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Simple Linear Regression  
  - Multiple Linear Regression  
  - Least Squares Estimation  
  - Assumptions of Regression Models  
  - Model Evaluation (R-Squared, Adjusted R-Squared)  
- **Practice**: Fit regression models to datasets, assess model performance  
- **Applications**: Predictive modeling, data analysis  

---

### 8. Time Series Analysis (Weeks 15-16)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Time Series Decomposition  
  - Autocorrelation and Partial Autocorrelation  
  - ARIMA Models (AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average)  
  - Forecasting Techniques  
- **Practice**: Apply time series models to real-world data  
- **Applications**: Forecasting in finance, stock market analysis  

---

### 9. Bayesian Inference (Weeks 17-18)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Bayes' Theorem Revisited  
  - Prior, Likelihood, and Posterior Distributions  
  - Conjugate Priors  
  - Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)  
- **Practice**: Perform Bayesian inference for model parameters  
- **Applications**: Machine learning, decision-making under uncertainty  

---

### 10. Markov Chains (Weeks 19-20)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Markov Processes and Transition Matrices  
  - Absorbing Markov Chains  
  - Stationary Distributions  
  - Monte Carlo Methods  
- **Practice**: Analyze and simulate Markov chains  
- **Applications**: Queueing theory, algorithm analysis  

---

### 11. Monte Carlo Methods (Weeks 21-22)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Random Sampling and Estimation  
  - Simulating Random Variables  
  - Variance Reduction Techniques  
  - Applications of Monte Carlo Methods  
- **Practice**: Implement Monte Carlo simulations for estimation problems  
- **Applications**: Simulation modeling, optimization  

---

### 12. Machine Learning Algorithms (Weeks 23-24)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Supervised Learning (Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, Decision Trees)  
  - Unsupervised Learning (K-Means, PCA)  
  - Overfitting and Regularization  
  - Evaluation Metrics (Accuracy, Precision, Recall)  
- **Practice**: Implement machine learning models using statistical methods  
- **Applications**: Artificial Intelligence, data-driven decision making-e
