## Syllabus  

### 1. Functions and Models (Weeks 1-2)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Four Ways to Represent a Function  
  - Essential Functions and Mathematical Models  
  - New Functions from Old Functions  
  - Graphing Techniques and Tools  
  - Exponential and Logarithmic Functions  
- **Practice**: Analyze and graph mathematical models; transform functions  
- **Applications**: Foundational for calculus concepts and engineering problems  

---

### 2. Limits and Derivatives (Weeks 3-4)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Tangent and Velocity Problems  
  - Understanding and Calculating Limits  
  - Precise Definition of a Limit  
  - Continuity and Discontinuity  
  - Derivatives: Concept, Rules, and Applications  
- **Practice**: Solve limit-based problems and derivative computations  
- **Applications**: Motion analysis, signal processing  

---

### 3. Differentiation Rules (Weeks 5-6)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Polynomial and Exponential Derivatives  
  - Product, Quotient, and Chain Rules  
  - Implicit and Logarithmic Differentiation  
  - Applications in Growth, Decay, and Related Rates  
- **Practice**: Solve real-world differentiation problems  
- **Applications**: Population growth models, physics simulations  

---

### 4. Applications of Differentiation (Weeks 7-8)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Maximum and Minimum Values  
  - Mean Value Theorem and Implications  
  - Curve Sketching and Optimization  
  - Newton’s Method for Roots  
  - Antiderivatives  
- **Practice**: Graph functions and solve optimization problems  
- **Applications**: Engineering design, optimization algorithms  

---

### 5. Integrals (Weeks 9-10)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Areas and Distances  
  - Definite and Indefinite Integrals  
  - Fundamental Theorem of Calculus  
  - Substitution Rule  
- **Practice**: Solve area-based and definite integral problems  
- **Applications**: Physics, engineering, and data analysis  

---

### 6. Techniques of Integration (Weeks 11-12)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Integration by Parts  
  - Trigonometric Integrals and Substitutions  
  - Partial Fractions  
  - Approximation Methods (e.g., Trapezoidal Rule)  
  - Improper Integrals  
- **Practice**: Apply advanced integration techniques  
- **Applications**: Complex engineering problems, approximation methods  

---

### 7. Applications of Integration (Weeks 13-14)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Areas Between Curves  
  - Volumes (Cross-Sections, Shells)  
  - Arc Length and Surface Area  
  - Work, Force, and Centers of Mass  
- **Practice**: Solve integration problems in engineering contexts  
- **Applications**: Structural analysis, robotics  

---

### 8. Differential Equations (Weeks 15-16)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Modeling and Solving Basic Differential Equations  
  - Direction Fields and Euler's Method  
  - Separable and Linear Differential Equations  
  - Population Growth Models  
  - Predator-Prey Systems  
- **Practice**: Model real-world phenomena with differential equations  
- **Applications**: Population studies, environmental modeling  

---

### 9. Advanced Topics (Weeks 17-20)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Vector Calculus (Green’s, Stokes’, Divergence Theorems)  
  - Multiple Integrals (Polar, Cylindrical, and Spherical Coordinates)  
  - Infinite Sequences and Series (Convergence Tests, Power Series)  
  - Parametric Equations and Polar Coordinates  
- **Practice**: Apply advanced calculus to multi-variable problems  
- **Applications**: 3D modeling, physics simulations  

---

### 10. Second-Order Differential Equations (Weeks 21-22)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Homogeneous and Non-Homogeneous Equations  
  - Applications in Physics and Engineering  
- **Practice**: Solve second-order differential equations  
- **Applications**: Circuit analysis, mechanical systems-e
