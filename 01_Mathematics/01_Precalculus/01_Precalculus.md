## Syllabus  

### 1. Algebra Foundations (Weeks 1-2)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Real Numbers, Exponents, and Radicals  
  - Algebraic Expressions and Rational Expressions  
  - Solving Equations and Inequalities (including graphical approaches)  
  - Introduction to Complex Numbers  
  - Coordinate Plane and Circles; Lines and Slopes  
  - Modeling Real-World Problems with Equations  
- **Practice**: Solve inequalities, simplify radicals, and explore linear models  
- **Applications**: Logical flow for programming, coordinate-based algorithms  

---

### 2. Functions and Transformations (Weeks 3-4)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Definition and Types of Functions (linear, quadratic, piecewise)  
  - Graphs and Transformations (shifts, reflections, stretches)  
  - Function Composition and Inverses  
  - Modeling with Linear and Nonlinear Functions  
- **Practice**: Plot functions using software tools; analyze real-world data for trends  
- **Applications**: Foundational for signal processing and computational modeling  

---

### 3. Polynomial and Rational Functions (Weeks 5-6)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Quadratic and Polynomial Functions  
  - Graphs, Real Zeros, and Rational Inequalities  
  - Division of Polynomials and Fundamental Theorem of Algebra  
- **Practice**: Explore polynomial curve fitting and rational function graphs  
- **Applications**: Control systems and approximation methods in engineering  

---

### 4. Exponential and Logarithmic Functions (Weeks 7-8)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Properties and Graphs of Exponential and Logarithmic Functions  
  - Solving Exponential and Logarithmic Equations  
  - Applications of Exponential Growth/Decay  
  - Logarithmic Scales and Data Transformation  
- **Practice**: Model financial growth or decay problems; logarithmic scaling  
- **Applications**: Signal amplitude measurement, algorithms for encryption  

---

### 5. Trigonometry and Vectors (Weeks 9-11)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Unit Circle and Trigonometric Graphs  
  - Right Triangle and Real Number Trigonometry  
  - Trigonometric Identities and Inverse Functions  
  - Introduction to Vectors and Dot Products  
- **Practice**: Solve triangle-related problems, graph sinusoidal waves, apply vectors to motion problems  
- **Applications**: Robotics, wave physics, and signal generation  

---

### 6. Matrices and Linear Systems (Weeks 12-13)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Matrix Operations, Inverses, and Determinants  
  - Solving Linear Systems using Matrices and Cramer’s Rule  
  - Systems of Nonlinear Equations and Inequalities  
- **Practice**: Solve multi-variable equations; explore basic computational methods  
- **Applications**: Circuit analysis, optimization problems, and linear algebra in machine learning  

---

### 7. Sequences, Series, and Limits (Weeks 14-15)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Arithmetic and Geometric Sequences  
  - Summation Notation and Mathematical Induction  
  - Introduction to Limits (numerical, graphical, and algebraic)  
  - Derivatives and Areas under Curves  
- **Practice**: Explore recursive patterns; solve limit-based problems  
- **Applications**: Basis for calculus, memory allocation patterns, and numerical analysis  

---

### 8. Conic Sections and Advanced Topics (Weeks 16-17)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Parabolas, Ellipses, and Hyperbolas  
  - Parametric Equations and Polar Coordinates  
  - Modeling Projectile Motion  
- **Practice**: Derive equations for real-world trajectories  
- **Applications**: Path optimization in robotics, image processing algorithms-e
