## Syllabus  

### 1. Vector Spaces and Subspaces (Weeks 1-2)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Definition and Properties of Vector Spaces  
  - Subspaces and Their Properties  
  - Linear Combinations and Span  
  - Null Space and Column Space  
  - Row Space and Rank  
- **Practice**: Solve problems on vector space axioms and subspace identification  
- **Applications**: Foundations for machine learning, data analysis, and network theory  

---

### 2. Linear Independence and Basis (Weeks 3-4)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Linear Independence of Vectors  
  - Basis and Dimension  
  - Row and Column Rank  
  - The Rank-Nullity Theorem  
- **Practice**: Determine the linear independence of sets of vectors, find bases for vector spaces  
- **Applications**: Optimization problems, image processing, computer graphics  

---

### 3. Matrix Operations (Weeks 5-6)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Matrix Multiplication and Properties  
  - Inverse and Transpose of Matrices  
  - Determinants and Their Computation  
  - Properties of Determinants  
  - Row Reduction and Echelon Forms  
- **Practice**: Perform matrix operations, compute determinants, and reduce matrices  
- **Applications**: Computational methods, graphics transformations, systems modeling  

---

### 4. Systems of Linear Equations (Weeks 7-8)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Solving Linear Systems with Gaussian Elimination  
  - Homogeneous and Non-Homogeneous Systems  
  - Matrix Formulation of Systems  
  - Consistency and Solution Spaces  
- **Practice**: Solve systems of equations, perform Gaussian elimination  
- **Applications**: Engineering problems, network analysis, cryptography  

---

### 5. Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors (Weeks 9-10)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Definition and Calculation of Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors  
  - Characteristic Polynomial  
  - Diagonalizability of Matrices  
  - Eigenvalue Decomposition  
- **Practice**: Compute eigenvalues and eigenvectors, diagonalize matrices  
- **Applications**: Principal Component Analysis (PCA), quantum computing, control theory  

---

### 6. Diagonalization (Weeks 11-12)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Diagonalization of Matrices  
  - Similarity Transformations  
  - Applications of Diagonalization in Differential Equations  
- **Practice**: Diagonalize matrices, apply diagonalization to solve systems  
- **Applications**: Simplifying matrix computations, systems theory  

---

### 7. Linear Transformations (Weeks 13-14)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Definition and Properties of Linear Transformations  
  - Matrix Representation of Linear Transformations  
  - Kernel and Image of a Transformation  
  - Change of Basis  
- **Practice**: Apply linear transformations, determine kernel and image  
- **Applications**: Computer graphics transformations, image processing  

---

### 8. Orthogonality and Least Squares (Weeks 15-16)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Inner Product Spaces  
  - Orthogonal Vectors and Subspaces  
  - Gram-Schmidt Process  
  - Least Squares Approximation  
- **Practice**: Solve problems involving orthogonality and apply the Gram-Schmidt process  
- **Applications**: Signal processing, optimization in machine learning  

---

### 9. Numerical Methods and Computational Linear Algebra (Weeks 17-18)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Numerical Solution of Linear Systems  
  - LU Decomposition  
  - Iterative Methods (Jacobi, Gauss-Seidel)  
  - Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)  
- **Practice**: Implement numerical methods to solve linear systems  
- **Applications**: Machine learning algorithms, engineering simulations  

---

### 10. Applications in Computer Engineering (Weeks 19-20)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Sparse Matrices and Their Applications  
  - Fourier Transforms and Signal Processing  
  - Matrix Factorization in Data Science  
  - Machine Learning Models and Linear Algebra  
- **Practice**: Solve real-world problems applying linear algebra techniques  
- **Applications**: Data science, machine learning, computer vision  

---

### 11. Advanced Topics in Linear Algebra (Weeks 21-22)  
- **Topics**:  
  - Matrix Exponentiation and Markov Chains  
  - Tensor Operations and Applications  
  - Advanced Eigenvalue Problems in Computational Systems  
- **Practice**: Apply advanced linear algebra methods to complex problems  
- **Applications**: Data science, advanced machine learning, artificial intelligence-e
